## The family budget

Create a budget with Type **Family** (see [Create a budget](/docs/budgets/create-a-budget)). Everyone in the family sees it; the Owner and Budget Admins edit it. On the Budgets page, the **Personal** / **Family** toggle switches between your own budgets and the family's.

Family savings goals work the same way and are funded by family budgets.

## Give someone their own budget, funded by the family

Say the family budget has $400 a month for Kids and you want your teenager to manage $150 of it themselves.

**From the family budget:** open the category's menu and choose **Make Personal Budget**. Pick who it's **For**, a **Budget Name** and the **Allocation**, then tap **Create Personal Budget**. The member now has their own budget, and that amount comes out of the family category.

**From the Create Budget sheet:** when a member creates their own budget, **Fund from your family** can draw from an **Existing category** or a **+ New family category**, or choose **No family funding**.

Only the Owner and Budget Admins can fund a budget from the family.

## Whose budget a card counts toward

On the **Accounts** page, each account in a family has a **Feeds** dropdown: **Family budget**, or a specific member. Pick your teenager for their debit card and its spending lands in their budget instead of the family's. The Owner and Budget Admins can change this.
