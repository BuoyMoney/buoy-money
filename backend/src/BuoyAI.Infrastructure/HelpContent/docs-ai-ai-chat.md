<span class="plan">Premium</span> and the free trial.

Open **AI Chat** from the menu and type a question in "Ask about your finances...". The AI answers from your real data in Buoy: accounts, transactions, budgets, count-up categories, savings goals, subscriptions, income and your rewards.

Good questions are specific: "How much did we spend eating out on weekends last month?", "Can I afford a $900 trip in March, and how?", "What subscriptions went up this year?"

Answers are for information only, not financial advice.

## Changes it can make, with your approval

The AI can also set things up for you. Before anything changes, it shows a **Confirmation Required** preview and waits for you to approve. It can:

- create or change budget categories, and switch rollover or count-up on or off
- create or update savings goals, and record money added to a goal
- add, delete, re-date or recategorize transactions

It has no way to move real money, pay anyone, or touch your bank.

## History

**Chat history** in the header shows your last 20 conversations. Open one to continue it, delete it, or start fresh. **Clear conversation** empties the current chat.

## Limits

Premium includes about 50 messages an hour and 200 a day. If you hit a limit, try again later.

:::note
AI chat currently needs your own Premium plan or trial. Family members who get Premium through their family can't use chat yet; that's being fixed.
:::

Before first use, AI features need to be on in **Settings → AI Assistant**. See [AI settings and memory](/docs/ai/ai-settings).
