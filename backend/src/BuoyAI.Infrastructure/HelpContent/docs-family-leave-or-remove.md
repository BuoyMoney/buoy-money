## Leave a family

Any member except the Owner can tap **Leave Family** on the Family Account card. The Owner gets an email. The Premium you had through the family ends when you leave, so you'll need your own plan to keep Premium features.

## Remove a member

The Owner can remove anyone from the member list. They get an email, and their family Premium ends the same way.

## Delete the family

Only the Owner can delete a family. Members, invitations and family categories are removed; budgets and savings goals are kept but no longer belong to a family.

:::caution[Known issue]
Deleting a family currently fails if a bank has ever been linked to it. A fix is on the way. If you need the family deleted now, email [contact@buoy-ai.com](mailto:contact@buoy-ai.com) and it will be done for you.
:::

## Linked accounts stay behind

Accounts and bank connections that joined the family stay attached to it after someone leaves or is removed. To stop a bank syncing into the family, disconnect it before they leave (the Owner or a Budget Admin can do it from **Accounts → ⋮ → Disconnect**). They can then link it again on their own account.

## Changing the Owner

There's no way to hand the Owner role to someone else in the app yet. If you need it, email [contact@buoy-ai.com](mailto:contact@buoy-ai.com).
