## AI features on or off

**Settings → AI Assistant → AI features (Google Gemini)** controls everything AI in Buoy: chat, insights and automatic categorization. Buoy's AI runs on Google's Gemini models.

- On the web and Android, AI features switch on the first time you use one.
- On iPhone, Buoy asks first with "Turn on AI features?".
- Switching it off stops all AI features on every device.

## AI Control

**AI Control** in Settings sets how hands-on the AI is: **Manual**, **Coach** (the default) or **Assistant**.

## Memory

With **AI Memory** on ("Allow AI to remember context from past conversations"), the AI picks up facts you mention, like an important date, a goal, a family detail or a spending habit, and uses them in later answers. It also uses your past conversations for context. AI Memory is **off** until you turn it on.

To see or change what it remembers, open **AI Memories** in the chat header. You can edit, pause or delete each memory, or **Delete All**.
