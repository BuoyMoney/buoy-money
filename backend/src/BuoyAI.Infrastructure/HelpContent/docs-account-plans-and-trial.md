## The free trial

Your free trial starts when you sign up, with full Premium features and no card. It lasts 14 days, or a month if you took the free-month offer on the website. One trial per email address. When it ends, Buoy asks you to choose a plan to keep going.

## Subscribe, switch or cancel

Open **Subscription & Billing** in the app.

- **Subscribe monthly** or **Subscribe yearly** opens a secure Stripe checkout, where you can also enter a promo code. On Android it opens in your browser.
- **Switch to monthly** or **Switch to yearly** changes your plan in place, with the difference prorated.
- **Manage Billing** opens Stripe's billing portal for your card and invoices.
- **Cancel Subscription** stops renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
