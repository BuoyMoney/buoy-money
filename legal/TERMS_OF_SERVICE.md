## 9. BUOYAI CLOUD

BuoyAI Cloud is optional. The self-hosted edition works without it.

### 9.1 What Cloud includes
- **Bank sync.** Delivers transactions from your bank logins, through Plaid, to your paired server. Three bank logins are included. You can buy extra bank logins, sold by quantity.

### 9.2 Pairing a server

### 9.3 Bank sync
You may connect only bank accounts that you own or are authorized to access. Cloud holds the Plaid access token for each bank login you connect, in encrypted form. When your server asks for new transactions, Cloud fetches them from Plaid, encrypts them so that only your server can open them, and sends them. Cloud does not store the transactions. Once your server has them, protecting them is your job (Section 8.3).

Bank data depends on Plaid and on your bank. It can be late, incomplete or interrupted, and Section 5.2 applies. Your plan has a limit on how many bank logins can sync. If you go over it, the newest logins stop syncing.
