## Push notifications (Android)

**Settings → Push Notifications → Notification Preferences.**

- **Enable Push Notifications** turns them all on or off.
- Individual switches: **Bill Reminders**, **Budget Alerts**, **Bank Connection Issues**, **Subscription Detection** and **Transaction Review Requests**.
- **Quiet Hours** holds notifications overnight.

If Buoy's switches are on but nothing arrives, check that notifications for Buoy are allowed in your phone's own settings.

## Email

**Settings → Email Notifications → Email Preferences.**

- **Always on**: login alerts and password changes, for your security.
- **Account activity**: bank connection problems and fixes, subscription changes, trial ending reminders.
- **Financial insights**: budget alerts, savings milestones, large transactions, monthly summary.
- **Achievements**: badges, milestones, streaks.
- **Marketing & updates**: new features, tips, product updates, offers.

Every email also has an unsubscribe link.
