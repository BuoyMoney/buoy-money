## In the app

Open **Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Account**. Type your email to confirm, and your password unless you sign in with Google.

## Without the app

Email [privacy@buoy-ai.com](mailto:privacy@buoy-ai.com) from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". It's done within 30 days. The [delete account page](/delete-account) has the details.

## What happens

- Any paid plan is cancelled and your billing record at Stripe is deleted.
- Bank connections are disconnected at Plaid.
- If you own a family, it's closed.
- Your data is deleted and your account record is anonymized.

Deleting an account doesn't give that email address a new free trial.
