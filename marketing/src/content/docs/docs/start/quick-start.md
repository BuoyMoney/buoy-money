Buoy works best in this order: account, security, bank, budget. Each step links to a longer guide.

1. **Create your account** at [buoy-ai.com/app](/app/register) or in the Android app. Sign up with email and password or with Google. Your free trial starts right away and there is no card to enter.
2. **Verify your email.** Buoy emails you a code; a banner on the dashboard stays up until you enter it. Bank linking needs a verified email.
3. **Turn on two-factor.** Banks require it before Buoy can link an account. A passkey or an authenticator app works best. See [Two-factor and security](/docs/account/security).
4. **Connect a bank** from the **Accounts** tab. Your last 30 days arrive within a minute and up to two years of history follow in the background. See [Connect a bank](/docs/banks/connect-a-bank).
5. **Create a budget** from the **Budgets** tab with **Create Budget**. Give each category an amount for the period. See [Create a budget](/docs/budgets/create-a-budget).
6. **Turn on Count-Up** for the categories where you tend to overspend, like dining out or shopping. Each one then earns a small allowance every day, and what you don't spend builds up. See [Count-up budgeting](/docs/budgets/count-up-budgeting).

After that, Buoy mostly runs itself. Transactions sync on their own, most get categorized automatically, and your streaks start counting.

:::tip
If you share money with a partner or family, one person with Premium creates the family first, then invites the others. See [Family sharing](/docs/family/family-sharing).
:::
