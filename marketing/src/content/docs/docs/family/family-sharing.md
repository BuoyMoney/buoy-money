A family puts a household's accounts, transactions and budgets in one place. The person who creates it needs <span class="plan">Premium</span>; everyone they invite gets Premium through the family and doesn't need their own plan.

## Create a family

Open **Profile** and find the **Family Account** card. Tap **Create Family**, give it a name, and you become its **Owner**. You can be in one family at a time.

## Invite someone

As the Owner, tap **Invite Member** on the Family Account card, enter their email, choose a role, and tap **Send Invitation**.

- They get an email with an **Accept Invitation** button. If they already use Buoy, they also get an in-app notification.
- They sign in (or create an account) with the **same email** you invited, then accept.
- Invitations expire after **7 days**. Pending ones are listed on the card with their role and time left; tap the X to cancel one.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including inviting, changing roles, removing members, renaming or deleting the family, and billing. There is one Owner. |
| **Budget Admin** | Create and edit family budgets and goals, give members their own family-funded budgets, link and disconnect banks, choose whose budget each account feeds. |
| **Member** | See everything, edit their own personal budgets and goals, categorize family transactions, add to family goals. Can't change family budgets or link banks. |
| **Read Only** | See everything, change nothing. |

The Owner changes roles from the member list. Nobody else can be made Owner.

## What everyone in the family can see

:::caution[Worth knowing before you invite]
Every member sees **all accounts linked to the family and every transaction on them**, whoever linked them. Family budgets are visible to everyone, and members can see each other's personal budgets (only the person who owns a personal budget can edit it). There is no private account inside a family.
:::

When someone accepts an invitation, the accounts, bank connections and savings goals they already had in Buoy join the family and become visible to everyone in it. What happens to their budgets depends on the family: if it already has a family budget, their budgets stay personal and are linked under it; if it doesn't, their budgets become family budgets.

If you want part of your spending to stay separate, keep it in a bank you don't link to Buoy, or use a separate Buoy account outside the family.
