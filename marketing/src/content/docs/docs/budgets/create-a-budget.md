Open the **Budgets** tab. With no budget yet you'll see **Create Budget**; tap it.

## The basics

- **Budget Name**, for example "Monthly Budget".
- **Period**: Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly (the default), Quarterly or Yearly. Category amounts are per period, so a Weekly grocery amount of $120 means $120 a week.
- **Type**: Personal, or Family if you're in a family. Family budgets need Premium; see [Family sharing](/docs/family/family-sharing).

## Categories

Add a row for each category and give it an amount.

- **Income categories** hold what you expect to earn in the period. Each shows **Expected** and **Earned**.
- **Expense categories** hold your spending limit.

Each expense row has two switches: **Rollover** carries what's left (or what you overspent) into the next period, and **Count-Up** turns the category into a daily allowance. Both are explained in [Count-up budgeting](/docs/budgets/count-up-budgeting) and [Rollover, transfers and ready to assign](/docs/budgets/rollover-and-transfers).

The sheet totals your Income, Expenses and what Remains as you type.

## After it's created

The menu at the top of the Budgets page has **Edit Budget**, **New Budget**, **New Category Group**, **Reset All Rollovers** and **Delete Budget**. Tap a category to edit it or for more actions.

A budget moves to its next period by itself. If you haven't opened Buoy in a while, it catches up through every period you missed the next time the budget loads, applying each category's rollover setting along the way.

:::tip
Spending shows up against a category once a transaction has that category. Most are sorted automatically; see [Automatic categorization](/docs/banks/automatic-categorization).
:::
