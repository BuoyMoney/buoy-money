Open the **Savings** tab and tap **New Goal**.

## Fields

- **Goal Name** and an optional **Description**.
- **Target Amount**, and **Amount Already Saved** if you've started.
- **Goal Type**: Personal, or Family if you're in a family (family goals need Premium).
- **Monthly Allocation**: a percentage or a fixed amount of your leftover budget that goes to this goal each period.
- **Held in account** (optional): the bank account the money actually sits in.
- **Count withdrawals toward budget**: whether taking money out of the goal counts as spending.

## Adding money

- **Add Funds** on a goal records a contribution.
- **Transfer to Savings** on a budget category moves that category's balance to a goal.
- A count-up category set to **Transfer to Savings** at period end sends its leftovers automatically.
- At the end of each period, every active goal also receives its **Monthly Allocation** share of what's left of your budget. Personal budgets fund personal goals and family budgets fund family goals.

Buoy records these amounts; it never moves money between your bank accounts.

## Tracking

The Savings page shows **Total Saved**, **Active Goals** and **Completed**. Open a goal to see Saved, Target, Remaining, the Target Date, and how much a day you'd need to reach it in time.
