Money Wrapped is a short recap of your month, in the style of a year-in-review.

## When it arrives

- **Monthly**: a notification during the first three days of each month, if you had activity the month before.
- **Yearly**: a notification during January 1 to 3.

You can also open it any time from the Money Wrapped card on the **Insights** page, for last month or "(year) so far".

## What it shows

- what you spent compared with the month before
- income and number of transactions
- your top category and top merchant
- zero-spend days and your biggest spending day
- your streak and badges earned
- money put toward savings goals

**Share it** makes an image you can send or post.
