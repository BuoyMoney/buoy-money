Find everything here under **Insights → Progress & Rewards**, and your badges under Achievements.

## Streaks

Buoy keeps five streaks: **Under Budget**, **Daily Login**, **Categorization**, **Savings** and **Family**. Under Budget counts the days you stay within budget. Daily Login counts the days you open Progress & Rewards, not just the app.

Count-up categories also show their own streak on each card.

## Freezes

Each full week you keep a streak going earns a freeze ❄️, and you can bank up to two. A freeze covers a missed day automatically, so one slip doesn't reset the count.

## Badges

Badges mark milestones, like **First Budget**, **Week Warrior** (7 days under budget), **Century Club** (100 days) and the **$100 Club**. There are also a few "oops" badges for the human moments, like **Time Traveler**.

## Levels

Points from good decisions move you up seven levels:

| Level | Points |
|---|---|
| Beginner | 0 |
| Saver | 100 |
| Budgeter | 300 |
| Expert | 600 |
| Master | 1,000 |
| Champion | 2,000 |
| Legend | 5,000 |

## Daily challenges

Tap **Get a Challenge** for a random one: **Categorization Champ** (+25 points), **Budget Check** (+15) or **No-Spend Day** (+30). **New Challenge** swaps it.
