Open the **Transactions** tab. The header shows income, expenses and net for the range you pick.

## Find a transaction

- The search box matches merchant and description.
- Date chips: All, 7D, 30D, 90D, 1Y, YTD.
- **Advanced Filters** add an amount range, custom dates, an account and a category.

## Edit one

Tap a transaction to open its details. From there you can:

- **Edit Category** (or tap the category chip directly)
- rename it, add notes and tags
- **Split** it across categories
- **Delete Transaction**

On a computer, right-click a row for the same menu.

## Split a purchase

For a Target run that was half groceries and half household, open the transaction and tap **Split**. Add a line per category with **Add Split**, or **Distribute Evenly**. **Unsplit Transaction** puts it back.

## Add cash or anything your bank missed

Tap **Add** to open "Add transaction". Choose Expense or Income, the amount, the category, and an account; pick **Cash (no account)** for cash. Notes are optional.

## Transfers

Moving money between your own accounts isn't spending. When Buoy spots likely transfers it shows a banner like "3 possible transfers counted as spending". Open it and choose **Mark as transfer** or **Not a transfer** for each. Confirmed transfers move to the Transfer category and drop out of your spending totals.
