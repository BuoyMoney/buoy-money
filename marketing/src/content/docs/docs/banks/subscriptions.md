Buoy looks through up to two years of your transactions for charges that repeat.

## How a subscription is found

A merchant becomes a suggestion when it charges a similar amount on a regular schedule at least three times, or twice about a year apart for annual plans. Detection runs weekly on its own. You can also tap **Detect** to run it now, then it rests for seven days (shown as "Cooldown").

On Premium, the AI also double-checks suggestions.

## What to do with a suggestion

- **Add** it to your subscriptions.
- **Snooze** it for a week up to three months.
- **Dismiss** it; it won't come back for 60 days.
- **Never suggest this merchant again**.

Suggestions you leave alone expire after 90 days.

## Possibly cancelled

A subscription that misses two or more billing cycles is flagged **Possibly Cancelled**. Choose **Keep Active** if it's still running (some charge irregularly), or **Cancel It** to mark it ended in Buoy. Buoy doesn't cancel anything with the merchant; you still do that with them directly.

For finding the ones you forgot about, see [How to find the subscriptions you forgot you were paying for](/blog/find-forgotten-subscriptions).
