A Walmart or Amazon charge arrives from your bank as one number. Send Buoy the receipt and it reads
the items, finds the matching charge and offers to split it: groceries in Groceries, paper towels in
Household, dog food in Pets. Receipt scanning is part of Premium.

## Send a receipt

- **From a store app (Android):** open the order in the Walmart, Amazon or other store app, take a
  screenshot, tap **Share** and choose **Buoy**. A long order can take a few screenshots; share them
  together.
- **A paper receipt:** in Buoy, open **Settings → Receipts → Scan a receipt** and take a photo.
- **From a transaction:** on a Walmart, Amazon, Sam's Club, Target, Costco or Instacart charge, tap
  **Scan receipt**. Buoy matches the receipt to that charge.

Buoy shows what you're sending before anything leaves your phone. Tap **Send to Buoy**, check the
split it suggests, and tap **Apply split**. Nothing changes until you do.

## If the charge isn't there yet

Orders are often charged when they ship or are delivered. If the charge hasn't come in from your
bank, Buoy keeps the receipt and lets you know when it matches, for up to two weeks.

## Privacy

Google Gemini reads what you send to find the store, items and prices. Buoy keeps those details and
the split, not the image. AI features need to be on. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
