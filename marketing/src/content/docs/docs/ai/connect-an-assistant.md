**Buoy Money Connect** lets Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants that support custom connectors (MCP) read your Buoy data, so you can ask "how's my grocery budget this month?" wherever you already chat.

## Add it

The connector address is:

```
https://buoy-ai.com/mcp
```

- **Claude**: Settings → Connectors → **Add custom connector**, and paste the address.
- **ChatGPT**: on a paid plan on the web, turn on Developer mode under Settings → Apps, then add the address as a connector.

The assistant opens a Buoy page titled "Connect (assistant) to Buoy Money". Check the account shown under "Signed in as", then tap **Approve**. You'll see "Connected" and a button back to the assistant.

## What it can do

It can **read** your accounts, transactions, spending summaries, budgets and count-up categories, cash flow, savings goals, subscriptions, income, your settings, your rewards, past Buoy chats, and these help pages.

It can **save a note** you ask it to remember, if AI Memory is on in Buoy.

It **can't** change budgets or transactions, and it can never move or spend money.

Questions from a connected assistant count toward the same hourly and daily AI limits as Buoy's own chat.

## Disconnect

Remove the Buoy connector in the assistant's settings. Buoy doesn't have its own list of connected assistants or a disconnect button yet.
