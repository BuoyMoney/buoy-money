<span class="plan">Standard</span> and up.

Buoy writes short insights about your spending, budgets and savings. You'll find them on the dashboard, alongside your budgets and savings, and on **Insights → AI Insights**.

You can refresh insights by hand up to 5 times a day on Standard and 15 on Premium.

For back-and-forth questions about your money, see [AI chat](/docs/ai/ai-chat), which is part of Premium.
