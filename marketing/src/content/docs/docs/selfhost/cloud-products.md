The self-hosted app is free and complete without these. BuoyAI Cloud sells a few add-ons for the parts a home server cannot do well on its own. Buy any combination, or none.

## Prices

| Product | Founding members | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|
| Bank sync (3 bank logins) | $23.99 a year or $2.79 a month | $35.99 a year or $4.29 a month |
| AI (monthly allowance included) | $14.99 a year or $1.99 a month | $26.99 a year or $3.49 a month |
| Bank sync and AI together | buy the two separately | $54.99 a year |
| Extra bank logins | $0.75 a month each ($9.00 a year) | $0.75 a month each ($9.00 a year) |

Prices are in US dollars.

## Founding members

People who reserved a founding spot for $1 before launch keep the founding prices. The $1 comes off their first payment. The one exception: if Plaid raises what it charges BuoyAI LLC per bank, that increase is passed on at cost.

## How billing works

- Everything you buy is on one subscription with one billing date, all monthly or all yearly.
- Adding a product starts it right away. The part of the period you have left is added to your next bill. On a yearly plan, those charges are billed monthly instead of waiting for the renewal.
- Removing a product ends it at the end of the period you paid for. There are no partial refunds.

## AI allowance

The AI product includes a monthly allowance. You get a notification and an email at 80% and at 100%. When the allowance runs out, AI features pause until the next month, unless your server's admin turns on pay as you go. Pay as you go keeps AI working up to a monthly limit the admin sets, and the extra use is added to the next bill.

## Instant refresh

Bank sync fetches new transactions on its own schedule. Instant refresh, which would let you ask for fresh data from your bank right now, is coming later. It is not for sale yet and has no price.

## Without Cloud

You can sync banks with [SimpleFIN](/docs/selfhost/simplefin) (paid to SimpleFIN directly) and run AI with your own provider key or a local model. Neither needs a BuoyAI Cloud account.
